# Crime scene, person inspection и body examination

**Status:** feature presence е `DECIDED`; rules са `PROPOSED/OPEN`; не е implemented.

## Player experience

Играчът влиза във focused location, наблюдава spatial relationships, examines selected objects/people, документира информация и си тръгва с непълно, но usable understanding. Scene-ът награждава attention, не exhaustive clicking.

## Proposed Small Crime Scene Prototype

- Една compact room.
- Едно body.
- 5–10 inspectable elements.
- 3–5 useful clues.
- Plausible irrelevant objects.
- Един NPC и едно contradiction.
- Basic evidence documentation.

## Person/body inspection

Възможни zones: face, eyes, hands, arms, clothes, shoes; head, neck, torso, limbs, pockets. Findings могат да са scratches, bruises, blood, mud, fibres, jewellery, wounds, defensive injuries или personal items. Странност не е automatic evidence.

## Must happen

- Spatial context има значение.
- Useful, irrelevant и ambiguous details coexist fairly.
- Inspection дава narrow observations, не conclusions.
- Real forensic concepts се research-ват преди factual portrayal.
- Progress не зависи от imperceptible pixels.

## Must not happen

- Всеки interactable е equally highlighted.
- Departure изисква click на всичко.
- Body zone автоматично дава cause/time of death без justified expertise.
- Gore без investigative value.
- Police/forensic procedure от memory или entertainment clichés.

## Open

Collection vs documentation; camera controls; permanent missability; scene departure rules; photography role; realism/chain-of-custody abstraction.
