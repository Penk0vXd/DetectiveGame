# Evidence, facts и statements

**Status:** conceptual distinctions са `DECIDED`; mechanics и data model са `OPEN`.

## Core distinction

```text
Source → Observation/Claim → Recorded knowledge → Player interpretation
```

Пример:

- Source: parking receipt.
- Fact: receipt показва `22:41` и location.
- Interpretation: suspect или vehicle може да е бил там.
- Unsupported leap: suspect лично е паркирал и е извършил murder-а.

## Categories

Physical objects, photos, documents, statements, calls/records, CCTV, forensic reports, fingerprints, blood, fibres, injuries, events и locations. Category не определя автоматично reliability.

## Proposed statement states

Unresolved, supported, contradicted, partially supported/contradicted, retracted/revised. Status не се поставя автоматично, освен при approved player verification action.

## Must happen

- Source и original content се пазят.
- Raw information е отделна от interpretation.
- Ambiguity и partial confirmation са възможни.
- Red herrings са fair и plausible.
- Review не пренаписва claim като truth.

## Must not happen

- `provesKiller` conclusion в evidence item.
- Statement тихо се превръща във fact.
- Contradiction автоматично доказва intentional lie.
- Reliability като universal visible percentage без validation.
- Всеки collected object става evidence.

## Open

Automatic recording; optional collection; permanent missability; manual notes; authored vs player-created links; provenance UI.
