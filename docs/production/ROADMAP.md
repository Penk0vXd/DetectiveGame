# Project roadmap

**Status:** phase order е `DECIDED`; detailed scope и dates са `OPEN`.

## 0. Concept Foundation

Vision, decisions/open questions, AI contract, prototype scope/tests, research и learning backlog.  
**Exit:** следващият prototype question е explicit.

## 1. Detective Office Prototype — current

**Question:** приятно и разбираемо ли е да обитаваш и използваш office-а като detective workspace?

Outputs: stable movement/modes; computer, phone, document и board flow; minimal connected information sequence; test/playtest records.  
**Exit:** functional P0 checks pass и Q-001 има playtest evidence.

## 2. Small Crime Scene Prototype

**Question:** observation и inspection създават ли fair reasoning в малка location?

Outputs: compact scene, body, inspectables, useful/irrelevant/ambiguous details, one NPC, one contradiction, basic evidence documentation.  
**Exit:** player interprets information без automatic guidance.

## 3. Investigation Systems Prototype

**Question:** могат ли sources, claims, evidence, board, timeline, dialogue и deduction да образуват coherent loop?

Outputs: minimal domain model, cross-source contradiction, chosen deduction approach и plausible wrong theory.  
**Exit:** player forms, revises и acts on theory.

## 4. Case 001 Vertical Slice

**Question:** може ли complete game promise да бъде delivered с representative quality?

Candidate scope е `PROPOSED`: office, one crime scene, 2–4 NPCs, 8–15 evidence items, phone/email/report, body examination, board/timeline/deduction и accusation за приблизително 20–40 минути.

**Exit:** end-to-end loop, stable content pipeline, blind playtest и production estimate.

## 5. Playtesting/iteration

Deduction ownership, fairness, ambiguity, interaction clarity, pacing, accessibility и scope cost.

## 6. Production

Започва само след validated core loop, case authoring, data model, save requirements и realistic content throughput.

Този roadmap определя dependencies, не release dates.
